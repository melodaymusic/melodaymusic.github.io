Meloday

A playlist that follows the clock

Seven times a day, from dawn to late night, Meloday builds a fresh mix from the songs you tend to play at that hour. It names the mix after what's in it and draws a cover with the sun where it would sit in the sky.

    • Named from your music. Titles come from the genres, moods and eras in the mix, like "Fitted Pop Saturday Evening".
    • Saved to Plex if you like. Keep it as one Plex playlist that updates in place, so Plexamp and your other apps see it too.
    • Ready when you are. It's made a couple of seconds after the app starts and changes on its own as the day goes on.

    Made for You

    Mixes made from what you actually play

    Daily mixes of about 30 songs follow your listening. Every Monday brings mood, energy and moment playlists, Genre Essentials for the genres you play most, and niche mixes named in two words about the music.

      • Every mix keeps going. Artist, genre, mood and sound mixes top themselves up as they run low, without repeating what's already queued.
      • Spread out on purpose. No artist twice within three songs, and songs Meloday has suggested a lot get a rest for a while.
      • One song, counted once. The album version, the deluxe edition and the remaster are treated as the same song.

      Live stations

      Stations that are already playing

      Meloday's live stations keep running while you're away, so tuning in drops you in mid-song, the way radio does. Meloday Pulse follows a daily schedule of programmes built from your library.

      Add your own programmes in the Pulse Guide. Schedule a song, artist, album, playlist, genre, mood or decade once, every day, on weekdays or on chosen days, for one song or an hour. And any song, album, artist, genre, style, mood or decade can start a radio of its own.

        Meloday Engine

        It listens to your songs

        The Meloday Engine analyses your music on your own PC and learns how each song sounds. Then you can search for a sound instead of a title.

        • Describes songs with 112 words for mood, energy, genre, instruments, vocals and moments.
        • Powers Sonic Radio, similar songs and Sonic Adventure, which travels from one song you pick to another.
        • Learns the sound of what you finish and what you skip, with a separate taste for mornings and late nights.
        • Nothing leaves your PC, and you don't need Plex Pass. If you have it, Plex fills in for songs the engine hasn't heard yet.

        AutoMix

        One song runs into the next

        AutoMix beat-matches the end of each song into the start of the next. Both songs meet halfway in tempo, so pairs up to about 21% apart still line up, and neither changes pitch.

        Highs, mids, then bass

        The next song's hi-hats come in first, then its mids. The bass lines swap on the downbeat halfway through, so two kick drums never fight.

        One voice at a time

        Where synced lyrics show singing, AutoMix lets one voice finish before the next begins. A vocal can ride over the next song's intro, then fade.

        A plan for every pair

        Songs too far apart to beat-match get a filter sweep, an echo out or a clean cut on a break. A look-ahead limiter keeps overlaps from distorting.

        Now Playing

        Something to watch while it plays

        Now Playing fills the window with the artist's backdrop, synced lyrics and a choice of visualizers that move with the music.

        • Lyric videos, with each word arriving as it's sung, in a typeface picked for the song.
        • Bars, Pillars and Tiles, plus projectM with five MilkDrop presets.
        • Synced lyrics from LRCLIB or Plex. If they're off, fix them in Lyric Studio by tapping the space bar as each word begins.

            FAQ

            Questions people ask

            Is there an iPhone or Android app?

            No, and there won't be. Plexamp already plays Plex music on phones and does it well, so a mobile version of Meloday Music wouldn't add anything.

            What about a Mac or Docker version?

            Those aren't planned either. I build Meloday Music around what I use every day, which is Plex on a Windows PC. I don't use a Mac, Android or Docker, so I couldn't make or test a version for them properly.

            Can someone else make one?

            Yes, please do. Meloday Music is open source under the GPL-3.0, so anyone is welcome to take the code to another platform. The source is on GitHub.

            Do I need Plex Pass?

            No. Everything works without it. If you have Plex Pass, Plex's own sonic analysis fills in for songs the Meloday Engine hasn't learned yet.

            Is it free?

            Yes, it's free and open source. If you'd like to support it, there's a Ko-fi page.

            Is it safe to install? Windows warned me about it.

            The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" and an unknown publisher. It shows that for any unsigned app from a small developer, and it doesn't mean anything was found in the file. Select More info, then Run anyway. To check your download first, compare its SHA-256 hash with the one in the release notes. All of the source is on GitHub if you'd rather read it or build the app yourself.

            Does my listening count in Plex?

            Yes, if you want it to. Meloday reports what you play back to your Plex server, so your play counts and history stay up to date in Plexamp and everywhere else you use Plex, and the song shows in Now Playing while it's on. It's on by default. If you'd rather keep your listening to yourself, turn off Report listening to Plex in Settings.

            Does my music or listening history leave my PC?

            The Meloday Engine analyses your songs on your PC, and the skips and choices it learns from stay there too. Your plays go to your own Plex server unless you turn that off, and nothing goes to Last.fm or ListenBrainz unless you connect them. Sign-ins and keys are encrypted with Windows DPAPI.

            Will it change my files or my Plex library?

            It never touches your audio files. Apart from reporting your plays, which you can turn off, it only writes to Plex when you ask it to, like saving a playlist, keeping Meloday on Plex or tagging moods. Moods it added can be removed in one step, and each song goes back to how it was.

            Does it work with Spotify or Apple Music?

            No. It plays the music in your Plex library, and everything it makes comes from there.

            What do I need to run it?

            Windows 10 version 2004 or later on a 64-bit PC, and a Plex Media Server with a music library.

            Is it made by Plex?

            No. Meloday Music is an independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Plex, Inc.

            Free, open source, made by one person

            Meloday started as a small Python script shared with Plexamp listeners on Reddit. This app grew out of it, built in spare time by someone who wanted a Plex player for Windows they'd enjoy every day.
